AIS Lab

A robotics research group at The University of Queensland, led by Dr Thien-Minh Nguyen.

We work on giving autonomous robots reliable perception and navigation in complex, large-scale environments: fusing complementary sensors for localisation and mapping, and coordinating teams of robots.

Research areas

  • Multi-sensor SLAMCamera, IMU, LiDAR and UWB fusion for accurate localisation and mapping.
  • Continuous-time estimationTrajectory representations for high-rate, asynchronous sensor data.
  • Multi-robot systemsCooperative localisation and control for teams of aerial and ground robots.
  • Datasets & benchmarksLarge-scale, open datasets such as MCD and NTU VIRAL for the research community.
